Many tuition teachers in Sri Lanka now teach online, at least part of the time. But running classes online often means juggling a lot of different tools: a WhatsApp group for announcements, Zoom links shared every week, recordings on Google Drive, tutes sent as PDFs, and payment slips arriving by photo.

It works, but it takes a lot of your time. And it's easy for students to miss something.

## What is an LMS?

An LMS (Learning Management System) is a website where everything for your classes lives in one place. Each student gets their own login, and they can see:

- **Live class links** for upcoming Zoom classes
- **Recordings** of past lessons, ready to watch again
- **Tutes and PDFs** to download
- **Quizzes and marks** so they can track their progress
- **Class fee status**, so they know what's paid and what's due

As the teacher, you get an admin dashboard where you can add lessons, check who has paid, and see how your students are doing.

## How it helps you

**You save time.** Instead of sending the same link or PDF again and again, you upload it once and every student in that class can see it.

**Payments are easier to track.** Students can pay their class fees online, and you can see who has paid without checking bank slips one by one.

**Only paid students get access.** You decide who can watch each recording, so your lessons stay with the students who enrolled.

**You look more professional.** A class with its own website and student portal builds trust with students and parents.

## How it helps your students

Students can catch up on a missed class at any time, find all their tutes in one place, and see their quiz marks. Parents can feel confident that their child has everything they need.

## Getting started

You don't need to be good with technology to run an LMS. Before you start, it helps to think about:

1. Which classes and subjects you want to put online
2. How you'd like students to pay (card, bank transfer or both)
3. Whether you'll upload recordings, run live classes, or both
4. Who will manage the admin side: you, or someone who helps you

> A good LMS should feel simple. If your students can use WhatsApp, they should be able to use your LMS.
