A new website is one of the best ways to bring in new customers. But the projects that go smoothest are the ones where the business owner has thought about a few things before the work starts.

Here's a simple checklist. You don't need everything to be perfect. Just having rough answers will help a lot.

## 1. Know what you want the website to do

Do you want more phone calls? WhatsApp messages? Online bookings? Product enquiries? Pick the one or two most important actions, and the whole website can be designed around them.

## 2. List the pages you need

Most small business websites need a Home page, an About page, a page for your services or products, and a Contact page. Write down any extra pages you need, such as a gallery, price list or FAQ.

## 3. Collect your content

Your website is only as good as what's on it. Start gathering:

- A short description of your business
- Details of your services or products
- Good-quality photos of your work, shop or team
- Your logo, if you have one

If you don't have good photos or a logo yet, that's fine. A designer can help you with both.

## 4. Choose a domain name

Your domain is your website address, like **yourbusiness.lk** or **yourbusiness.com**. Keep it short, easy to spell and easy to say over the phone.

## 5. Find a few websites you like

Look at websites from other businesses, in Sri Lanka or abroad, and note what you like about them: the colours, the layout, how easy they are to use. This helps your designer understand your taste quickly.

## 6. Decide how customers will contact you

Most Sri Lankan customers prefer a quick WhatsApp message or a phone call. Make sure the right numbers are ready, and decide who will reply to enquiries from the website.

## 7. Think about who will update it

Will you want to change prices, add photos or post news yourself? If so, ask for an easy admin panel so you can update the website without any coding.
